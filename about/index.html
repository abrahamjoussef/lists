Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There’s nothing to buy and no ads.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in Safari or Chrome. Tap Share › Add to Home Screen to keep it one tap away.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — any modern phone browser, and on computers too.

Who can see my lists?

Only you and the people you invite. You choose whether each person can edit or only view, and you can remove them any time.

What if there’s no signal in the supermarket?

Keep ticking. The app saves changes on your phone and syncs them when you are back online.
