Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There’s nothing to buy and no ads.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in Safari or Chrome. Tap Share › Add to Home Screen to keep it one tap away.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes — any modern phone browser, and on computers too.

Who can see my lists?

Only you, and the people you share each list with. For every list you choose No access, View or Edit for each person, and you can change it any time.

What if there’s no signal?

Keep going. The app saves changes on your phone and syncs them when you are back online.

Can I delete lists, even the main one?

Yes. Tap ✎ on any list, then Delete. Only the list’s owner can do this. You can delete every list and start fresh with “+ Create a list”.

What kinds of lists can I make?

Shopping, to-do, packing and event lists. Each type has its own sections and suggestions, and you can change a list’s type any time with ✎.
